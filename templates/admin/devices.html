How a node joins

A Pi running remote_lab_pi posts to /api/node/register at boot with its ID, IP and experiment slug, then sends telemetry to /api/node/heartbeat every 30 seconds. It goes offline here after 90 seconds of silence. Adding by IP does the same thing from this side — the portal calls GET /api/info on the node and stores what comes back.
