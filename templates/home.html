Real hardware.
Booked by the hour.

The IKEN Remote Lab gives students exclusive, scheduled access to physical microcontroller benches over the internet — flash your own firmware, watch the serial stream, and see the board on camera while it runs.

◉
One bench, yours alone

A booking reserves a specific rig. Nobody else holds the serial port while your slot is running.

⌁
Your own firmware

Upload a compiled .bin, .hex or .out, flash it to the board, and plot sensor values live.

◷
Access ends with the slot

Each session carries a key that expires when your hour does. The bench resets for whoever is next.
